    Controls

    Move: WASD or arrows. Jump: space. To bunny hop, press jump again as you land and strafe in the air (A or D while turning that way). Slide or crouch: Shift, the only way through the low holes in walls. Ladders: walk into them. Power: E. Whistle: T (everyone hears which direction it came from). Menu: Esc.

    Game setup

    Only the host can change these. They apply to everyone in the room.
